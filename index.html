Zyvraon products

What ZYVRAON builds

An ethical fintech platform, plus three open platforms that anyone can join, not just the company.

Halal Street

Fintech · India
Halal Street by ZYVRAON

An ethical fintech platform for Indian investors. It brings clear, institution-grade Shariah-compliant stock screening, portfolio management and purification calculations to everyday investors.

  • Helps Indian Muslim investors find halal stocks with confidence.
  • Live at halalstreet.vercel.app.

Zyvraon App Store

Android · APK
Also known as Hamza's Playstore

An independent Android app store run by Zyvraon. Developers can list their apps and users download them directly as APK files. It is also how every Zyvraon app, including Hamza Music, reaches its users.

  • Open to developers: anyone can publish their app.
  • APK files and store images are served from the public hamzas-playstore-assets repository.

Hamza Music

v2.1.150 · build 150
Also released as Rox Music

A music streaming app for Android, built in Flutter. Listeners stream songs for free, and artists can upload their own music to reach new listeners.

  • Open to artists: anyone can upload their own original songs.
  • More than 30 public releases, from v2.1.0 to v2.1.150.
  • Plays in the background, with sponsor ads from Zyvraon Ad Network since v2.1.138.

Zyvraon Ad Network

Video + audio ads
Also known as Zyvraon Ads

Zyvraon's own advertising network. Brands, local businesses and creators can run sponsor ads inside Zyvraon apps and partner apps, with no middleman.

  • Open to advertisers: anyone can run an ad.
  • Video ads in the player and audio ads while music plays in the background.
  • Built and run fully by Zyvraon, separate from third-party ad networks.
