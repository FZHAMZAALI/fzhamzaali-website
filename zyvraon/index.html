What is ZYVRAON?

ZYVRAON (ZYVRAON®) is a software company from Gorakhpur, Uttar Pradesh, India. It was founded and is led by Hamza Ali (fzhamzaali), who is its Founder & CEO. ZYVRAON began as a D2C audio brand and grew into a software and ethical fintech company. It builds its own products end to end, from mobile apps to the servers and ad systems behind them.
